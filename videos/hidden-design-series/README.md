Each folder also has an `.srt` captions file.

**EP.05–09 are the funnier batch.** They're longer (33–44 s) and add:
- a recurring character with elbows and hands, so they can mash buttons, facepalm, panic or celebrate
- a second voice (Kokoro `am_puck`) for the character's one-liners: "Come on, come on!", "Just one. Real quick.", "I'm keeping the grape."
- speech bubbles, stamped punchlines ("FAKE!", "NOPE.", "GENIUS!") and full backgrounds (elevator, crosswalk, office, plane cabin, bus, 1800s frontier, kitchen)

Suggested posting order: **05 → 04 → 09 → 06 → 01 → 07 → 03 → 08 → 02**. Lead with the strongest "wait, what?" hooks, and alternate funny episodes with explainers.

### EP.05 Placebo buttons
- **Title:** That elevator button you keep pressing? It might be fake 😳 #shorts
- **Description:** In many elevators, the "door close" button does nothing for regular riders. In the US, accessibility rules make doors stay open long enough for everyone, so the button often only works with a firefighter's key.
  A 2004 report found most New York crosswalk buttons did nothing, and some office thermostats are dummies. They're called placebo buttons.

- **Title:** Smoking is banned on planes… so why is there an ashtray? ✈️🚬 #shorts
- **Description:** Aviation rules still require an ashtray near every airplane lavatory door. Engineers know someone will break the rules anyway,
  and a cigarette in a bin full of paper towels is a fire risk. Smoking on a plane can still get you a big fine.

### Fact notes for EP.05–09 (check these before you publish)
- **EP.05:** NYT (2016) reported many US elevator door-close buttons are inoperable for passengers because the ADA (1990) requires doors to stay open long enough. Fire-service mode uses a key.
  NYT (2004) reported most of New York's crosswalk buttons were non-functional. "Dummy" office thermostats have been reported by HVAC contractors (e.g. WSJ, 2003). The video hedges with "many", "most" and "some".
- **EP.06:** US FAA rule 14 CFR 25.853 requires lavatory ashtrays on or near the entry side of each lavatory door, regardless of whether smoking is allowed. The reasoning is that a cigarette discarded in a waste bin is a fire hazard. Fines for smoking on board are real and can be large; the video gives no amount.
- **EP.07:** Transit-seat "moquette" patterns are chosen to hide dirt, stains and wear and to make vandalism less visible. The video makes no specific numeric claims.
- **EP.08:** Levi's describes the small pocket as originally designed for pocket watches (it is often called the watch pocket). The video only says "many of them" carried watches.
- **EP.09:** Many foil and wrap boxes print "press here to lock end" on the end tabs, which push into the roll's tube to stop it sliding out. The video says "many" and "often".

## Fact notes (check these before you publish)
- **Padlock:** many padlocks have a drain/weep hole that lets water out (preventing rust and freezing), and it can be used for lubrication. Not every padlock model has one.
- **Airplane window:** windows have an outer pane, a middle pane with a small breather hole, and an inner scratch pane. The hole equalizes pressure,
  so the outer pane bears the cabin-pressure load and the middle pane is a fail-safe. It also vents moisture to reduce fogging/frost.
- **Fuel gauge:** the arrow (or triangle) next to the pump icon shows the fuel-door side on most modern cars. The video says "in most cars" because it isn't universal.

## Rebuilding / making new episodes
From this folder, with the skill installed (`npm ci` in `skills/directing-stickman-videos`):

```bash
cd ../hidden-design-epNN-topic/source
python3 ../../hidden-design-series/tts.py /path/to/kokoro-models narration.wav af_heart   # Kokoro v1.0 onnx + voices
python3 ../../hidden-design-series/cues.py                                               # captions.json from real audio
node ../../hidden-design-series/make.mjs <skillDir> .                                     # project.json (CLEAN=1 disables TV finish for QA)
node <skillDir>/scripts/autopilot.mjs preview "$PWD/project.json" --seconds <duration> --replace
ffmpeg -i output/preview.mp4 -vf pad=1080:1920:0:40:color=0x111316 -af loudnorm=I=-14:TP=-1.5 ... final.mp4
```

- `lib.mjs` holds the shared vector library: series chrome, people, padlock, plane window, gauge, top-down car, rain, end card.
- An episode is `script.json` (one entry per sentence, with caption chunks) plus `episode.mjs` (one scene function per sentence;
  it receives the shot length and the caption start times, so animation lands on the spoken words).
- `make.mjs` turns each sentence into one shot, times everything from the actual narration, and rejects captions that would wrap.

Voice: Kokoro `af_heart` (Apache-2.0), the same voice in every episode. The skill's default (Gemini *Bodi*) needs a Gemini API key, which wasn't available.

### EP.05+ additions
- `script.json` entries can set `"voice": "am_puck"` (any Kokoro voice) for a character line. The default narrator stays `af_heart`.
- `lib.mjs` v2 adds `actor()` (jointed arms solved automatically from hand targets such as `'face'`, `'up'`, `'hips'` or `[x, y]`, plus hats and jeans legs), `bubble()`, `stamp()`, `sweat()`, `marks()`, `hop()`, `jitter()` and the five-episode `endCard2()`.
- `make.mjs` masks anything drawn outside the stage panel (EP.05+), and drops duplicate keyframe times.
- `node frame.mjs <skillDir> <module.mjs> out.png [t]` renders a single still of a layer list, which is handy for pose and prop design checks.
- `bash qa.sh <episodeDir> sheet.png` makes a contact sheet with one frame per shot from `output/preview.mp4`.
