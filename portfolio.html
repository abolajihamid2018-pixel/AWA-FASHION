About

Dressed in purpose, born in Africa

I believe African style is not a trend — it's a legacy. My work is making that legacy wearable every day, anywhere in the world.

I started AWA Fashion to blend the continent's rich textile traditions with clean, contemporary silhouettes: clothing that speaks before you do, from boardrooms in Lagos to runways in Paris.

I work across the whole brand — shaping the collection, setting the visual direction, and building the online store that lets customers browse, pay and order in the way that suits them.

Philosophy

Dress with purpose. Dress with power.

Fit is everything

No fabric can rescue a poor fit. Tailoring is the most powerful style upgrade there is.

Embrace your roots

African prints and textures are global fashion currency. One Ankara accent can lift the most classic outfit.

Quality over quantity

Five exceptional pieces outperform twenty mediocre ones. Buy less, choose well.
